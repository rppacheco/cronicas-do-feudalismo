# Arte do mapa isométrico

Ferramenta: geração de imagens integrada, sem uso de CLI ou API externa.

Arquivo: `dist/assets/mapa-feudo-isometrico.png`.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: illustrated background for an educational interactive web map. Create a wide landscape 5:3 isometric medieval manor game map strongly inspired by the visual rendering of Ultima VIII Pagan (1994): richly textured old PC RPG pixel art, realistic proportions, raised isometric stone buildings, detailed sprites, muted olive moss, brown soil, grey stone, rust tile roofs, directional afternoon shadows, intricate pixel shading and dithering. NOT flat 8-bit tiles, NOT vector, NOT a modern smooth 3D render. Entire map visible from elevated isometric view without horizon. Layout for eight clickable landmarks: grey fortified castle and courtyard upper left centered 25% x 29% y; stone church with cross and bell tower center at 49% x 43% y; a village cluster of three or four timber houses lower center-left at 39% x 65% y; lord's golden grain fields upper middle-right at 64% x 29% y; peasant strip crops lower middle-right at 65% x 68% y; common pasture with a few sheep lower left at 23% x 82% y; a watermill with visible water wheel middle right at 81% x 59% y; dense woodland upper right at 84% x 24% y. Narrow river flows near right edge north to south with bridge by the mill. Connect buildings with winding earth and cobblestone paths. Include clearly visible medieval people rendered as detailed isometric game sprites with recognizable clothing: 7 villagers farming or carrying baskets near village and fields, 3 nobles in richly colored burgundy and blue cloaks near castle courtyard, 3 clergy in long brown or black robes and one pale stole by church. People must be visible and sufficiently large, each around 3% of image height, distinct groups, appropriate to medieval Western Europe. Lively, grounded inhabited environment, no monsters, no magic, no combat. Grass and rocky natural border fill the whole frame, no empty white margins. No lettering, no numbers, no UI, no labels, no watermarks. Original composition, no copied game assets. Polished game environment background.
