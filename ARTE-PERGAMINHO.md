# Pergaminho envelhecido

Arte criada com a geração de imagens integrada. Arquivo: `dist/assets/pergaminho-envelhecido.png`. Transparência preservada. A moldura usa nove regiões em CSS para adaptar a área central sem deformar as extremidades.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent vertical UI background for the sidebar menu of a medieval educational game. Draw ONE tall narrow unrolled ancient parchment scroll, front-facing straight on, portrait aspect ratio approximately 1:3.2, fully visible, centered, filling almost the full image height and width with a tiny transparent margin. Hand-drawn ink contours and painterly watercolor gouache textures reminiscent of illustrated 1990s isometric medieval PC RPG inventory panels. Warm aged ivory central writing area, ochre and sepia dark weathered edges, natural freckles and foxing, subtle folds, slightly ragged uneven sides with tiny nicks and tears. Clearly recognizable paper scroll curls at BOTH top and bottom: irregular rolled paper with visible spiral cross sections at both ends, hand-inked crease lines, overlapping folded paper lips. Not wooden rods, not metallic cylinders, not shiny smooth gradients. Strongly illustrated paper surface, not photorealistic. The central 70% of width and 78% of height must remain pale and quiet for readable dark text overlaid in HTML. No writing, no text, no symbols, no ornament inside the writing area. Plenty of aged paper personality along edges. Modest illustrated shadow confined to the object. Completely transparent outside the paper, no background, no surrounding scenery. Entire complete standalone scroll, not cropped.
